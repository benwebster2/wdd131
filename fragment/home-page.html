The Premise of Fragment

The galaxy is more broken than ever before. Despite the Neverending Conference’s best efforts, order and peace are nonexistent in most of the galaxy. Their alliances with other alien races are on glass bridges that are waiting to break. Humanity is stretched far too thin, and a lurking evil has noticed. With attacks from this unknown enemy, the Conference deploys its soldiers to protect and serve the planets they inhabit. But it’s becoming clear that this is no ordinary threat, but one that is hellbent on destroying humanity and the other alien races. Seemingly because they deem them unfit to inhabit such a glorious galaxy. The Conference will not allow itself to be cast into darkness again. They have called upon all of their allies to fight against this enemy, putting together every possible piece to help save them. With these forces fighting against each other, a galaxy-wide war has erupted, and we shall see who shall win.
